BIHAR GIG AND PLATFORM WORKERS (SOCIAL SECURITY AND WELFARE) ACT, 2024
(Act No. 12 of 2024)
[Received the assent of the Governor on 1st March, 2024]

An Act to provide for the social security and welfare of gig workers and platform workers in the State of Bihar,
constitution of a Gig Workers Welfare Board, mandatory registration of aggregators and workers,
social security fund with aggregator contributions, and for matters connected therewith or incidental thereto.

CHAPTER I: PRELIMINARY

Section 1: Short title, extent and commencement
(1) This Act may be called the Bihar Gig and Platform Workers (Social Security and Welfare) Act, 2024.
(2) It extends to the whole of the State of Bihar.
(3) It shall come into force on such date as the State Government may, by notification in the Official Gazette, appoint.

Section 2: Definitions
In this Act, unless the context otherwise requires:
(a) "aggregator" means a digital platform that connects gig workers with customers for providing services;
(b) "gig worker" means a person engaged in work through a digital platform, outside traditional employer-employee relationship;
(c) "platform worker" means a gig worker providing services through a digital platform;
(d) "Welfare Board" means the Bihar Gig and Platform Workers Welfare Board constituted under section 4;
(e) "Social Security Fund" means the fund constituted under section 8.

CHAPTER II: REGISTRATION

Section 3: Mandatory registration of aggregators
(1) Every aggregator operating in Bihar shall register with the Welfare Board within sixty days of commencement.
(2) Registration certificate shall be displayed prominently on the platform.

Section 4: Constitution of Bihar Gig and Platform Workers Welfare Board
(1) The State Government shall constitute the Bihar Gig and Platform Workers Welfare Board.
(2) Composition: Chairperson (Minister-in-charge), Vice-Chairperson, Government officials, representatives of aggregators, gig workers, and experts.

Section 5: Registration of gig workers
(1) Every gig worker shall register with the Welfare Board through the online portal.
(2) Registration shall be free, Aadhaar-based, and linked to e-Shram UAN.
(3) A unique Gig Worker ID shall be issued upon registration.

Section 6: Registration of platforms
All digital platforms engaging gig workers in Bihar shall register and share worker data with the Welfare Board.

CHAPTER III: SOCIAL SECURITY FUND AND CONTRIBUTIONS

Section 7: Social Security Fund
There shall be constituted the Bihar Gig and Platform Workers Social Security Fund.

Section 8: Sources of the Fund
The Fund shall consist of:
(a) aggregator contributions;
(b) nominal worker contributions (where applicable);
(c) State Government grants;
(d) income from investments;
(e) any other receipts.

Section 9: Aggregator contributions
(1) Every aggregator shall contribute such percentage of the transaction value or worker earnings to the Fund as notified.
(2) The contribution rate shall be between one to two percent of the transaction value.
(3) Contributions shall be deposited monthly.

Section 10: Utilization of Fund
The Fund shall be utilized for:
(a) life and disability insurance;
(b) health insurance and medical benefits;
(c) maternity benefits;
(d) children's education scholarships;
(e) skill development and certification;
(f) housing and emergency assistance;
(g) administrative expenses of the Board.

CHAPTER IV: WELFARE MEASURES

Section 11: Welfare schemes
The Welfare Board shall notify schemes for:
(a) accident and disability cover (minimum Rs. 5 lakh);
(b) health insurance cover (minimum Rs. 2 lakh per annum);
(c) maternity benefit (minimum Rs. 50,000);
(d) pension for workers above 60 years;
(e) educational assistance for children;
(f) skill upgradation and vocational training.

Section 12: Portability of benefits
Benefits shall be portable across platforms and aggregators within Bihar and shall be linked to the worker's unique ID.

Section 13: Linkage with e-Shram and Central schemes
The Board shall ensure seamless integration with e-Shram portal and facilitate enrolment in PM-SYM, PMJJBY, PMSBY, Ayushman Bharat, and other Central schemes.

CHAPTER V: GRIEVANCE REDRESSAL

Section 14: Internal Grievance Redressal by aggregators
(1) Every aggregator shall establish a time-bound Internal Grievance Redressal Mechanism.
(2) The mechanism shall resolve grievances within thirty days.
(3) A dedicated Grievance Redressal Officer shall be appointed.

Section 15: Welfare Board Grievance Redressal
(1) Aggrieved gig workers may approach the Welfare Board if not satisfied with aggregator's resolution.
(2) The Board shall dispose of complaints within forty-five days.
(3) The Board may order compensation, reinstatement, or penalties.

Section 16: Protection against arbitrary deactivation
(1) No aggregator shall deactivate a gig worker without:
   (a) written grounds communicated to the worker;
   (b) prior notice of at least fourteen days;
   (c) opportunity of hearing.
(2) Deactivation for safety/critical reasons requires post-facto justification within 48 hours.

Section 17: Dispute resolution timeline
All disputes shall be resolved within a maximum of ninety days from filing.

CHAPTER VI: OFFENCES AND PENALTIES

Section 18: Penalties for non-compliance
(a) Non-registration of aggregator: Up to Rs. 5,00,000.
(b) Non-payment of contributions: Twice the amount due plus interest.
(c) Arbitrary deactivation: Up to Rs. 3,00,000 and mandatory reinstatement.
(d) Non-compliance with grievance redressal: Up to Rs. 2,00,000.
(e) Failure to share worker data: Up to Rs. 1,00,000.

Section 19: Compounding of offences
Offences may be compounded by the Welfare Board on payment of specified amounts.

Section 20: Cognizance of offences
Only on complaint by Welfare Board or authorized officer.
