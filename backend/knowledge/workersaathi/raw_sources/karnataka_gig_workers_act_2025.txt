KARNATAKA PLATFORM-BASED GIG WORKERS (SOCIAL SECURITY AND WELFARE) ACT, 2025
(Act No. 22 of 2025)
[Received the assent of the Governor on 30th May, 2025]

An Act to provide for the social security and welfare of platform-based gig workers in the State of Karnataka,
constitution of a Welfare Board, mandatory registration, fourteen-day notice before deactivation,
internal dispute redressal, and for matters connected therewith or incidental thereto.

CHAPTER I: PRELIMINARY

Section 1: Short title, extent and commencement
(1) This Act may be called the Karnataka Platform-based Gig Workers (Social Security and Welfare) Act, 2025.
(2) It extends to the whole of the State of Karnataka.
(3) It shall be deemed to have come into force on the 30th day of May, 2025.

Section 2: Definitions
In this Act, unless the context otherwise requires:
(a) "aggregator" means a person who owns, operates or manages a digital platform that connects platform-based gig workers with customers;
(b) "platform-based gig worker" means a person who performs work through a digital platform and earns from such work outside a traditional employer-employee relationship;
(c) "platform" means a digital application or website used by an aggregator;
(d) "Welfare Board" means the Karnataka Platform-based Gig Workers Welfare Board constituted under section 4;
(e) "Internal Dispute Redressal Officer" means the officer appointed under section 10.

CHAPTER II: REGISTRATION

Section 3: Registration of aggregators
(1) Every aggregator operating in Karnataka shall register with the Welfare Board within sixty days of commencement.
(2) Registration shall be valid for five years and renewable.

Section 4: Constitution of Karnataka Platform-based Gig Workers Welfare Board
(1) The State Government shall constitute the Karnataka Platform-based Gig Workers Welfare Board.
(2) The Board shall be a body corporate with perpetual succession.
(3) Composition: Chairperson (Minister, Labour), Vice-Chairperson, Government officials, aggregator representatives, gig worker representatives, and experts.

Section 5: Registration of platform-based gig workers
(1) Every platform-based gig worker in Karnataka shall register with the Welfare Board.
(2) Registration shall be free, online, and linked to e-Shram UAN.
(3) A unique Gig Worker Identification Number (GWIN) shall be issued.

Section 6: Obligations of aggregators
(1) Aggregators shall share details of engaged gig workers with the Welfare Board monthly.
(2) Aggregators shall display registration certificates on their platforms.

CHAPTER III: SOCIAL SECURITY AND WELFARE

Section 7: Welfare Fund
There shall be a Karnataka Platform-based Gig Workers Welfare Fund.

Section 8: Contributions to the Fund
(1) Aggregators shall contribute a welfare fee as notified by the State Government.
(2) The fee shall be a percentage of transaction value or worker earnings.
(3) Contributions shall be deposited monthly.

Section 9: Welfare schemes
The Welfare Board shall notify schemes for:
(a) Life and disability insurance;
(b) Health insurance and medical benefits;
(c) Maternity benefits;
(d) Children's education assistance;
(e) Skill development and certification;
(f) Pension for workers above 60 years;
(g) Emergency financial assistance;
(h) Housing support.

Section 10: Portability of benefits
Benefits shall be portable across platforms within Karnataka and linked to the GWIN.

CHAPTER IV: GRIEVANCE REDRESSAL AND DISPUTE RESOLUTION

Section 11: Internal Dispute Redressal Officer
(1) Every aggregator shall appoint an Internal Dispute Redressal Officer (IDRO).
(2) The IDRO shall be accessible through the platform, email, and phone.
(3) The IDRO shall acknowledge grievances within 48 hours.

Section 12: Dispute resolution timeline
(1) The IDRO shall resolve grievances within fourteen days of receipt.
(2) If not resolved within fourteen days, the gig worker may approach the Welfare Board.
(3) The Welfare Board shall dispose of the dispute within thirty days.

Section 13: Protection against arbitrary deactivation
(1) No aggregator shall deactivate a gig worker's ID without:
   (a) Written grounds communicated to the worker;
   (b) Fourteen days' prior written notice;
   (c) Opportunity of hearing before the IDRO.
(2) Exception: Immediate deactivation for safety or fraud, with post-facto explanation within 48 hours.
(3) Deactivation without compliance with sub-section (1) shall be deemed arbitrary and illegal.

Section 14: Appeal to Welfare Board
A gig worker aggrieved by the IDRO's decision or arbitrary deactivation may appeal to the Welfare Board within thirty days.

Section 15: Welfare Board powers
The Welfare Board may:
(a) Order reinstatement of deactivated IDs;
(b) Award compensation;
(c) Impose penalties on aggregators;
(d) Direct compliance with welfare schemes.

CHAPTER V: OFFENCES AND PENALTIES

Section 16: Penalties
(a) Non-registration of aggregator: Up to Rs. 5,00,000.
(b) Non-payment of welfare fee: Twice the amount due plus interest at 18% per annum.
(c) Arbitrary deactivation without 14-day notice: Up to Rs. 5,00,000 and mandatory reinstatement.
(d) Failure to appoint IDRO: Up to Rs. 2,00,000.
(e) Non-compliance with dispute resolution timeline: Up to Rs. 3,00,000.
(f) Non-sharing of worker data: Up to Rs. 1,00,000.

Section 17: Compounding of offences
Offences may be compounded by the Welfare Board on payment of specified amounts.

Section 18: Cognizance of offences
No court shall take cognizance except on complaint by the Welfare Board or authorized officer.
