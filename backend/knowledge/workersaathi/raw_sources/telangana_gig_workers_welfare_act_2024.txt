TELANGANA GIG AND PLATFORM WORKERS WELFARE ACT, 2024
(Act No. 8 of 2024)
[Received the assent of the Governor on 1st June, 2024]

An Act to provide for the welfare and social security of gig workers and platform workers in the State of Telangana,
constitution of a Welfare Board, mandatory insurance coverage, enrollment in State Welfare Directory,
and for matters connected therewith or incidental thereto.

CHAPTER I: PRELIMINARY

Section 1: Short title, extent and commencement
(1) This Act may be called the Telangana Gig and Platform Workers Welfare Act, 2024.
(2) It extends to the whole of the State of Telangana.
(3) It shall come into force on such date as the State Government may, by notification in the Official Gazette, appoint.

Section 2: Definitions
In this Act, unless the context otherwise requires:
(a) "aggregator" means a person who owns, operates or manages a digital platform for connecting gig workers with service recipients;
(b) "gig worker" means a person who performs work through a digital platform and earns from such work outside traditional employment;
(c) "platform worker" means a gig worker engaged via a digital platform for delivery, transportation, or other services;
(d) "Welfare Board" means the Telangana Gig and Platform Workers Welfare Board constituted under section 4;
(e) "Welfare Directory" means the digital registry of gig workers and aggregators maintained under section 7.

CHAPTER II: REGISTRATION AND WELFARE BOARD

Section 3: Registration of aggregators
(1) Every aggregator shall register with the Welfare Board within sixty days of commencement.
(2) Registration shall include details of the platform, number of workers, and business model.

Section 4: Constitution of Telangana Gig and Platform Workers Welfare Board
(1) The State Government shall constitute the Telangana Gig and Platform Workers Welfare Board.
(2) The Board shall be a body corporate with perpetual succession.
(3) Composition: Chairperson (Minister, Labour Department), Vice-Chairperson, Government nominees, aggregator representatives, worker representatives, and experts.

Section 5: Registration of gig workers
(1) Every gig worker in Telangana shall register with the Welfare Board.
(2) Registration shall be Aadhaar-linked and integrated with e-Shram.
(3) A unique Worker Welfare ID shall be issued.

Section 6: Mandatory enrollment in Welfare Directory
(1) All aggregators shall enroll their active gig workers in the State Welfare Directory within thirty days of engagement.
(2) The Directory shall be updated in real-time.

Section 7: State Welfare Directory
(1) The Welfare Board shall maintain a digital Welfare Directory of all registered gig workers and aggregators.
(2) The Directory shall be accessible to workers, aggregators, and government departments.

CHAPTER III: SOCIAL SECURITY AND INSURANCE

Section 8: Mandatory insurance coverage
Every registered gig worker shall be entitled to:
(a) Accidental Insurance Cover: Minimum Rs. 5,00,000 (five lakh rupees);
(b) Health Insurance Cover: Minimum Rs. 10,00,000 (ten lakh rupees) per annum for the worker and family.

Section 9: Insurance implementation
(1) The Welfare Board shall empanel insurance companies through transparent bidding.
(2) Premium shall be shared between the aggregator, worker, and State Government as notified.
(3) Aggregators shall deduct and remit the worker's share (if any) and pay their share directly.

Section 10: Coverage for delivery and transport workers
Delivery partners, ride-hailing drivers, and logistics workers shall be prioritized for insurance enrollment.

Section 11: Claims process
Insurance claims shall be processed through a dedicated portal with a target settlement time of fifteen days.

CHAPTER IV: WELFARE FUND AND CONTRIBUTIONS

Section 12: Welfare Fund
There shall be a Telangana Gig and Platform Workers Welfare Fund.

Section 13: Contributions to the Fund
(1) Aggregators shall contribute such percentage of transaction value as notified (1-2%).
(2) Contributions shall be deposited monthly.
(3) The State Government may provide matching grants.

Section 14: Utilization of Fund
The Fund shall be used for:
(a) Insurance premium subsidies;
(b) Skill development and certification programs;
(c) Financial literacy and digital training;
(d) Emergency financial assistance;
(e) Welfare Board administrative expenses.

CHAPTER V: GRIEVANCE REDRESSAL AND PROTECTIONS

Section 15: Internal Grievance Redressal
(1) Every aggregator shall establish an Internal Grievance Redressal Mechanism with a designated officer.
(2) Grievances shall be resolved within twenty-one days.
(3) The mechanism shall be accessible via app, web, and toll-free helpline.

Section 16: Welfare Board Grievance Redressal
(1) Unsatisfied workers may approach the Welfare Board.
(2) The Board shall dispose of complaints within thirty days.
(3) Powers include ordering compensation, reinstatement, and imposing penalties.

Section 17: Protection against arbitrary deactivation
(1) No aggregator shall deactivate a worker's ID without:
   (a) Written grounds;
   (b) Fourteen days' prior notice (except safety emergencies);
   (c) Opportunity of hearing.
(2) Deactivated workers may appeal to the Welfare Board for reinstatement.

Section 18: Algorithmic transparency
Aggregators shall disclose key parameters of work allocation, rating, and deactivation algorithms to the Welfare Board annually.

CHAPTER VI: OFFENCES AND PENALTIES

Section 19: Penalties
(a) Non-registration of aggregator: Up to Rs. 5,00,000.
(b) Non-enrollment in Welfare Directory: Rs. 10,000 per worker per month of default.
(c) Failure to provide insurance: Up to Rs. 3,00,000 and mandatory enrollment.
(d) Arbitrary deactivation: Up to Rs. 5,00,000 and reinstatement order.
(e) Non-compliance with grievance redressal: Up to Rs. 2,00,000.

Section 20: Compounding
Offences may be compounded by the Welfare Board.

Section 21: Cognizance
Only on complaint by Welfare Board or authorized officer.

CHAPTER VII: MISCELLANEOUS
