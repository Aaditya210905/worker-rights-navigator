RAJASTHAN PLATFORM BASED GIG WORKERS (REGISTRATION AND WELFARE) ACT, 2023
(Act No. 18 of 2023)
[Received the assent of the Governor on 24th July, 2023]

An Act to provide for the registration of platform based gig workers, aggregators and primary employers, 
constitution of a Welfare Board, social security and welfare measures for platform based gig workers 
in the State of Rajasthan and for matters connected therewith or incidental thereto.

CHAPTER I: PRELIMINARY

Section 1: Short title, extent and commencement
(1) This Act may be called the Rajasthan Platform Based Gig Workers (Registration and Welfare) Act, 2023.
(2) It extends to the whole of the State of Rajasthan.
(3) It shall come into force on such date as the State Government may, by notification in the Official Gazette, appoint.

Section 2: Definitions
In this Act, unless the context otherwise requires:
(a) "aggregator" means a digital intermediary or a marketplace for a buyer or user of a service to connect with the seller or the service provider;
(b) "gig worker" means a person who performs work or participates in a work arrangement and earns from such activities outside of traditional employer-employee relationship;
(c) "platform" means an online platform or application used by an aggregator to connect gig workers with customers;
(d) "welfare cess" means the cess levied under section 12;
(e) "Welfare Board" means the Rajasthan Platform Based Gig Workers Welfare Board constituted under section 5.

CHAPTER II: REGISTRATION

Section 3: Registration of aggregators
(1) Every aggregator operating in the State shall register itself with the Welfare Board within sixty days from the date of commencement of this Act.
(2) The registration shall be valid for a period of five years and renewable thereafter.

Section 4: Registration of gig workers
(1) Every gig worker working through a platform in the State shall register with the Welfare Board.
(2) On registration, a unique Social Security ID shall be assigned to each gig worker.
(3) The Social Security ID shall be portable across platforms and valid throughout the State.

Section 5: Constitution of Welfare Board
(1) The State Government shall constitute a Board to be called the Rajasthan Platform Based Gig Workers Welfare Board.
(2) The Board shall consist of a Chairperson, representatives of the State Government, aggregators, gig workers, and experts.

Section 6: Functions of the Welfare Board
The Welfare Board shall:
(a) register aggregators and gig workers;
(b) administer the welfare fund;
(c) formulate welfare schemes for gig workers;
(d) provide for grievance redressal mechanism;
(e) monitor compliance by aggregators.

CHAPTER III: SOCIAL SECURITY AND WELFARE

Section 7: Welfare schemes
The Welfare Board may notify schemes for:
(a) life and disability cover;
(b) health and maternity benefits;
(c) old age protection;
(d) skill development and training;
(e) any other welfare measure as may be notified.

Section 8: Welfare Fund
(1) There shall be constituted a Fund to be called the Rajasthan Platform Based Gig Workers Welfare Fund.
(2) The Fund shall consist of:
   (a) welfare cess collected from aggregators;
   (b) contributions from gig workers;
   (c) grants from the State Government;
   (d) any other receipts.

Section 9: Welfare cess
(1) Every aggregator shall pay welfare cess at such rate, not exceeding two per cent of the value of each transaction, as may be notified by the State Government.
(2) The cess shall be collected and remitted to the Welfare Fund.

Section 10: Social security contributions
Aggregators shall contribute to the social security of gig workers as per the schemes notified by the Welfare Board.

CHAPTER IV: GRIEVANCE REDRESSAL

Section 11: Grievance Redressal Mechanism
(1) Every aggregator shall establish an Internal Grievance Redressal Mechanism for gig workers.
(2) The aggregator shall resolve grievances within twenty-one days from the date of receipt.
(3) If the grievance is not resolved within twenty-one days, the gig worker may approach the Welfare Board.

Section 12: Appellate Authority
The State Government shall constitute an Appellate Authority to hear appeals against orders of the Welfare Board.

CHAPTER V: OFFENCES AND PENALTIES

Section 13: Penalty for non-registration
If an aggregator fails to register under section 3, it shall be liable to a penalty which may extend to five lakh rupees.

Section 14: Penalty for non-payment of cess
If an aggregator fails to pay welfare cess, it shall be liable to a penalty equal to twice the amount of cess due.

Section 15: Penalty for non-compliance with grievance redressal
If an aggregator fails to comply with the grievance redressal mechanism under section 11, it shall be liable to a penalty which may extend to five lakh rupees.

Section 16: Cognizance of offences
No court shall take cognizance of any offence under this Act except on a complaint made by the Welfare Board or an officer authorised by it.

CHAPTER VI: MISCELLANEOUS

Section 17: Power to make rules
The State Government may make rules for carrying out the provisions of this Act.

Section 18: Power to remove difficulties
If any difficulty arises in giving effect to the provisions of this Act, the State Government may make such provisions as appear necessary.
