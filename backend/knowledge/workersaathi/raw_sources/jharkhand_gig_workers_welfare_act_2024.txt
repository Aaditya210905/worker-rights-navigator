JHARKHAND GIG WORKERS WELFARE ACT, 2024
(Act No. 5 of 2024)
[Received the assent of the Governor on 15th February, 2024]

An Act to provide for the welfare and social security of gig workers and platform workers in the State of Jharkhand,
constitution of a Welfare Board, registration of gig workers and aggregators, and for matters connected therewith or incidental thereto.

CHAPTER I: PRELIMINARY

Section 1: Short title, extent and commencement
(1) This Act may be called the Jharkhand Gig Workers Welfare Act, 2024.
(2) It extends to the whole of the State of Jharkhand.
(3) It shall come into force on such date as the State Government may, by notification in the Official Gazette, appoint.

Section 2: Definitions
In this Act, unless the context otherwise requires:
(a) "aggregator" means a person who owns, manages or controls a digital platform for connecting gig workers with customers;
(b) "gig worker" means a person who performs work or participates in a work arrangement through a digital platform and earns from such activities outside of traditional employer-employee relationship;
(c) "platform worker" means a gig worker engaged through a digital platform;
(d) "Welfare Board" means the Jharkhand Gig Workers Welfare Board constituted under section 4.

CHAPTER II: REGISTRATION

Section 3: Registration of aggregators
(1) Every aggregator operating a digital platform in the State shall register with the Welfare Board within ninety days from the date of commencement of this Act.
(2) Registration shall be valid for three years and renewable.

Section 4: Constitution of Jharkhand Gig Workers Welfare Board
(1) The State Government shall constitute the Jharkhand Gig Workers Welfare Board.
(2) The Board shall consist of a Chairperson, officials from Labour Department, representatives of aggregators, gig workers, and civil society.

Section 5: Registration of gig workers
(1) Every gig worker in the State shall register with the Welfare Board through the designated portal.
(2) On registration, a unique identification number shall be assigned.
(3) Registration shall be free of cost and valid for the duration of engagement as a gig worker.

Section 6: Registration of platforms
All digital platforms engaging gig workers in the State shall register with the Welfare Board and provide details of gig workers engaged.

CHAPTER III: WELFARE MEASURES

Section 7: Welfare Fund
(1) There shall be a Jharkhand Gig Workers Welfare Fund.
(2) The Fund shall receive contributions from aggregators, gig workers, and State Government grants.

Section 8: Welfare schemes
The Welfare Board may notify schemes for:
(a) accident insurance and disability cover;
(b) health insurance;
(c) maternity benefits;
(d) children's education assistance;
(e) skill upgradation and certification;
(f) housing assistance;
(g) any other scheme as notified.

Section 9: Contributions by aggregators
(1) Aggregators shall contribute such percentage of transaction value or worker earnings to the Welfare Fund as notified.
(2) The contribution rate shall not exceed two per cent of the transaction value.

Section 10: Benefits portability
Benefits under this Act shall be portable across platforms and aggregators within the State.

CHAPTER IV: GRIEVANCE REDRESSAL AND DISPUTE RESOLUTION

Section 11: Internal Grievance Redressal
(1) Every aggregator shall establish an Internal Grievance Redressal Mechanism.
(2) Grievances shall be acknowledged within 48 hours and resolved within thirty days.
(3) The mechanism shall be accessible through the platform and a dedicated helpline.

Section 12: Welfare Board Grievance Redressal
(1) If a grievance is not resolved by the aggregator within thirty days, the gig worker may approach the Welfare Board.
(2) The Welfare Board shall dispose of the grievance within forty-five days.
(3) The Welfare Board may direct the aggregator to pay compensation or reinstate the gig worker.

Section 13: Protection against arbitrary deactivation
(1) No aggregator shall deactivate a gig worker's account without:
   (a) a valid reason communicated in writing;
   (b) an opportunity of hearing;
   (c) at least fourteen days' prior notice.
(2) Emergency deactivation for safety reasons shall be followed by a detailed explanation within 48 hours.

CHAPTER V: SOCIAL SECURITY

Section 14: Social security coverage
The Welfare Board shall endeavour to provide:
(a) life and disability insurance coverage;
(b) health insurance for gig workers and their families;
(c) old age pension scheme;
(d) maternity benefits for women gig workers.

Section 15: Linkage with Central schemes
The Welfare Board shall facilitate enrolment of eligible gig workers in Central Government schemes such as e-Shram, PM-SYM, PMJJBY, PMSBY, and Ayushman Bharat.

CHAPTER VI: OFFENCES AND PENALTIES

Section 16: Penalties
(1) Failure to register: Penalty up to Rs. 2,00,000 for aggregators.
(2) Non-payment of contributions: Penalty equal to twice the amount due.
(3) Arbitrary deactivation: Penalty up to Rs. 3,00,000 and order for reinstatement.
(4) Non-compliance with grievance redressal: Penalty up to Rs. 2,00,000.

Section 17: Compounding of offences
Offences under this Act may be compounded by the Welfare Board on payment of specified amounts.
