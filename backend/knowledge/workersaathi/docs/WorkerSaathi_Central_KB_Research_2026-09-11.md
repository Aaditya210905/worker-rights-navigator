## SECTION A — Executive summary

1. The four Labour Codes **commenced 21 November 2025** (PIB PRID 2206144; Gazette S.O. 5319–5322(E)).
2. **Central implementing Rules** were notified **8 May 2026**: G.S.R. 342(E) IR, 343(E) Wages, 344(E) Social Security, 345(E) OSH&WC.
3. **Exception:** SS Code s.164(1) item 3 (repeal of EPF Act 1952) was **not** commenced by S.O. 5319(E). Treat EPF Act 1952 as still in force unless a later Gazette is verified.
4. **Unpaid wages (employees):** minimum wage is universal (not only scheduled employments); deductions capped at 50%; overtime at least 2×; claims in **Form-II** under Wages (Central) Rules 2026 Rule 49 / Code s.45 (Central sphere).
5. **Gig/platform workers** are legally recognised **outside** the traditional employer-employee relationship. Central law gives **social-security framework + registration**, **not** a verified reinstatement right after app deactivation, and **not** a verified Code-on-Wages employee wage claim.
6. **Workplace injury:** employer must notify Inspector-cum-Facilitator (OSH Central Rules 2026 Rule 7, FORM-XI). ESI disablement/dependants benefits apply only if covered (wage ceiling **Rs 21,000/month** “at present” — Additional FAQs Q12). PMSBY is a separate bank-account accident insurance (Rs 20/year).
7. **e-Shram** is the national unorganised-worker database (31.78 crore as on 14.07.2026). Registration is **free**, **not a cash benefit** (FAQ Q42). Helpline **14434 / 18008896811**.
8. **Operational Central schemes** with verified eligibility/benefits: **PM-SYM** (Rs 3000 pension, 18–40, income ≤ Rs 15000), **PMJJBY** (Rs 2 lakh death, 18–50, Rs 436/year), **PMSBY** (accident 18–70, Rs 20/year), **APY** (18–40, not tax-payer), **PM-JAY** (SECC occupational list includes domestic and construction workers; Rs 5 lakh/family/year).
9. **Legal aid:** NALSA s.12 — industrial workman and **women irrespective of income**; helpline **15100**.
10. **Do not use** Shram Suvidha as a worker grievance portal. **Do not** treat SAMADHAN FAQ old-Act names as current law.

India Code Act PDFs returned 404/500 from this sandbox. Section numbers below that come only from Rules (which cite the parent Act) or from MoLE FAQs/explainer are labelled accordingly.

## SECTION C — Central Labour Acts (operative meaning for MVP)

### C1. Code on Wages, 2019 (Act 29 of 2019) — commenced 21.11.2025 (S.O. 5322(E))

**Applicability (official explanations):** universal minimum wages to **all employments** (not only scheduled employments). Timely payment applies to **all employees** (Additional FAQs Q2). Gig/platform workers are defined elsewhere as **outside** employer-employee relationship — do **not** auto-apply this Code to them.

**Extracted operative points** (section numbers: Rules + FAQs + explainer; India Code PDF not downloaded):

| Topic | What the official source says | Cite |
|---|---|---|
| Minimum wage | Employer legally prohibited from paying less than prescribed minimum wage | Additional FAQs Q3 |
| Floor wage | Statutory; Central Government to notify; State minima shall not be less | PIB 2206144 |
| Floor wage amount | **NOT_FOUND** | — |
| Wages definition s.2(y) | Effective 21.11.2025; 50% add-back of items (a)–(i); OT in the 50% calc; gratuity/ESI not in that 50% basket | Additional FAQs Q1, Q6, Q7 |
| Equal pay | No gender discrimination in pay for same/similar work or in recruitment (includes transgender per PIB) | Explainer s.3; PIB 2206144 |
| Payment timelines | Daily: end of day; weekly: last working day; fortnightly: before 2nd day after fortnight; monthly: before 7th of succeeding month | Explainer (TIER 3). **Section number conflict** — explainer says s.6; Rules use s.6 for minimum-wage calculation |
| Exit pay | All due wages within **two working days** of resignation/dismissal/termination | Explainer cites **s.17** |
| Deductions | Only authorised; **≤ 50%** of wages; excess carried forward (Rule 13 / s.18(4)) | Rules 13, 16–19 |
| Overtime | ≥ **twice** normal rate if minimum wage is fixed under the Code | Explainer; Additional FAQs Q5, Q24 |
| Claims | Form-II before s.45 authority; group claims allowed; union or Inspector-cum-Facilitator may file | Rule 49 |
| Appeal | Form-III to s.49 appellate authority; employer must deposit claim amount | Rule 50 |
| Wage slip | Form V on or before payment | Rule 52 / s.50(3) |

**Who is NOT shown as covered:** gig/platform workers as employees. Domestic workers in private households: **UNVERIFIED**.

### C2. Code on Social Security, 2020 (Act 36 of 2020) — partially commenced 21.11.2025 (S.O. 5319(E))

**In force:** ss.1–14; 15(1)(2); 16(1)(c); 17–141; 143 except S.O.2060(E) item (v); 144–163; s.164(1) items 1,2,4–9; 164(2)(a)(c) and 164(3).

**NOT commenced:** s.164(1) **item 3** (EPF Act 1952 repeal).

**Gig/platform (PIB 9 Dec 2025 + Rules 48–49):**
- Definitions: aggregator / gig worker / platform worker / platform work (work **outside** traditional employer-employee relationship).
- Aggregator contribution **1–2% of annual turnover, capped at 5%** of amounts paid/payable — **specific % still “to be notified”** (Additional FAQs Q15).
- Registration on designated Central portal (in practice e-Shram) under s.113; aggregators must API-share workers within 45 days of Rules and thereafter real-time/daily, including exits.
- Scheme eligibility days: **90 days** with one aggregator or **120 days** cumulative across aggregators in last FY; one calendar day with income = one day; three aggregators same day = three days.
- Cease eligibility at **age 60** or if days condition not met (Rule 49(4)).
- Benefits contemplated (life/disability, health/maternity, pension, accident insurance, crèche) are **government-notified scheme** benefits — **not** automatic PF/ESI as an employee.
- s.45: Central Government **may** notify ESIC Chapter IV schemes for unorganised/gig/platform workers and families.

**ESI (employees):** mandatory if establishment has **>10** employees; voluntary if <10; even **1 employee** if hazardous process; pan-India. Wage ceiling **Rs 21,000/month** until further notification (Additional FAQs Q12). Benefits listed in explainer s.32: sickness, maternity, disablement (employment injury), dependants’, medical, funeral.

**Gratuity:** FTE eligible after **one year** of contract (Additional FAQs Q14). Calculation on revised wages from **21.11.2025**. Contractor (not principal employer) pays gratuity for contract labour (Q16 citing s.53).

**Building workers:** Rule 45 — age **18 to <60**, **≥90 days** construction work in preceding 12 months; State Board benefits (State-specific amounts **not imported**); portability to destination State Board.

### C3. OSH & WC Code, 2020 (Act 37 of 2020) — commenced 21.11.2025 (S.O. 5321(E); canonical standalone Gazette PDF **not independently fetched** this session)

Employer accident-notice duties are implemented in **OSH (Central) Rules 2026 Rule 7** (extracted from Gazette PDF on labour.gov.in):

- **Death:** FORM-XI **forthwith** to Inspector-cum-Facilitator; also telephone/electronic to Chief Inspector-cum-Facilitator, DM/SDO, police station **and family/kin**.
- **Injury preventing work ≥48 hours:** FORM-XI within **12 hours after those 48 hours**, electronic, to Inspector-cum-Facilitator.
- **Dangerous occurrence:** intimation within **12 hours** to Inspector-cum-Facilitator and DM/SDO.

Construction (MoLE explainer, TIER 3 s.18, 24, 78): free temporary accommodation; no employment of persons known/reasonably believed to have hearing impairment, defective vision, or giddiness in operations that may pose accident risk; comply with Central BoCW OSH standards.

Women: may work in all establishments; night work (before 6:00 a.m. or after 7:00 p.m.) requires **consent** and prescribed safety (explainer s.43).

Working hours/OT (Additional FAQs Q24): **8 hours/day**; OT at **2×** if >8 hours (daily wager) or >48 hours/week; paid at end of wage period.

### C4. Industrial Relations Code, 2020 (Act 35 of 2020) — commenced 21.11.2025 (S.O. 5320(E))

**GRC (s.4; IR Central Rules 2026 Rules 6–8):**
- Industrial establishment with **20 or more workers** must constitute GRC; equal employer/worker members; **max 10**; women representation at least proportionate; term **3 years**.
- Worker application within **1 year** of cause of action; GRC to finish in **30 days**.
- If aggrieved or unresolved: apply to **conciliation officer** within **60 days** via Trade Union, online on MoLE designated portal or speed post/in person.

Works Committee (explainer s.3): establishments with **100+** workers may be required to constitute.

Standing Orders (explainer): **300+** workers.

**Gig deactivation is not shown as an IR industrial dispute in the extracted Rules.**

## SECTION D — Central Labour Rules (8 May 2026)

All four are **RULES**, in force from Gazette publication **2026-05-08**, applying where **Central Government is appropriate Government** (unless a rule is wider).

Worker-facing forms/deadlines extracted:

**Wages (Central) Rules, 2026**
- Rule 49 Form-II: wage/discrimination claims
- Rule 50 Form-III: appeal (employer deposits claim amount)
- Rule 52 Form-V: wage slip
- Rule 13: 50% deduction ceiling
- Rules 16–18: 7-day show-cause before fine / absence deduction / damage deduction
- Rule 5: 8-hour normal day (daily wage period); 48-hour week otherwise
- Rule 4: VDA using Labour Bureau CPI-IW before 1 April and 1 October

**Social Security (Central) Rules, 2026**
- Rule 45: building worker registration (18–<60; 90 days)
- Rule 48: unorganised + gig/platform registration (age 16+)
- Rule 49: aggregator Form-XX (provisional by **30 June**), Form-XXI (final by **31 October**); interest **1% per month** on delay
- Rule 49(4): gig scheme eligibility ceases at 60 or if 90/120-day test fails

**OSH (Central) Rules, 2026**
- Rule 7 + FORM-XI: accident/dangerous-occurrence notice (see C3)

**IR (Central) Rules, 2026**
- Rules 6–8: GRC composition, worker application, escalation to conciliation

**Last update on Objectives page:** 11-Sep-2026

**Who:** unorganised worker (home-based, self-employed, or wage worker in unorganised sector, or organised-sector worker **not** in ESIC/EPFO and not a government employee). Includes construction, migrant, gig/platform, street vendors, domestic, agriculture.

**Need:** Aadhaar + Aadhaar-linked mobile (or CSC biometric). **Free. No last date. 24×7.** UAN 12-digit, permanent. Card never expires.

**Age conflict:** Objectives/PIB: 16+; FAQ Q11: 16–59. Flag both.

**Benefit of card:** facilitates delivery of schemes; **not automatic cash** (FAQ Q42).

## SECTION F — Central worker / social-security schemes

### PM-SYM (pension)
- Authority: MoLE. Portal: https://maandhan.in
- Age **18–40**; monthly income **≤ Rs 15,000**; not EPFO/ESIC/NPS (Central Govt.) and not income-tax assessee.
- Contribution **Rs 55–200**/month (by entry age); **50% worker + 50% Central Government**.
- Benefit: **Rs 3,000/month** from age 60; spouse 50% on death. Enrolment >54 lakh as on 29.07.2026. Pension payments cannot start before Feb 2039 for 2019 joiners (PIB 2293891).

### PMJJBY (life)
- DFS. Age **18–50**; bank/PO account; auto-debit; one policy.
- Premium **Rs 436/year** (pro-rata if joining mid-year as per PIB table).
- **Rs 2 lakh** on death due to any cause; **30-day lien**.
- Forms: https://www.jansuraksha.gov.in/Forms-PMJJBY.aspx

### PMSBY (accident)
- DFS. Age **18–70**; bank/PO account; auto-debit.
- Premium **Rs 20/year**.
- Death / full disability **Rs 2 lakh**; specified partial **Rs 1 lakh**.
- Forms: https://jansuraksha.gov.in/Forms-PMSBY.aspx
- This is **not** ESI and **not** employer accident compensation.

### APY
- Age **18–40**; bank account; **not income-tax payer**.
- Pension **Rs 1,000–5,000**/month from 60 by contribution choice.

### AB-PM-JAY
- NHA. **Rs 5 lakh/family/year** hospitalisation (PIB Aug 2025).
- SECC 2011 rural deprivation + urban occupations **including domestic worker and construction worker**.
- Platform-worker-specific PM-JAY: Budget 2025-26 announcement; Aug 2025 PIB said **yet to be launched**. Status on 2026-09-11: **CURRENT_STATUS_UNCONFIRMED** (nha.gov.in / pmjay.gov.in 503).

e-Shram One-Stop mapping **does not create eligibility**. Each scheme’s own test applies.
